How to Convert a Word Document to PDF Without Surprises

By Zainab Fuzail · Updated November 2026

You wrote a CV or an assignment in Word and the website asks for a PDF. A PDF looks the same on every phone and computer, and nobody can change it by accident. This guide shows how to convert a .docx file in your browser and what to check afterwards.

When Do You Need a PDF Instead of Word?

  • Job applications: a CV in PDF keeps its layout on every device. See our guide on preparing a job application PDF.
  • Assignments: many teachers and portals accept PDF only.
  • Forms and letters: a PDF cannot be changed by accident after you send it.
  • Printing: the pages print the way you saw them on screen.

If you are not sure which format to choose, read our comparison PDF vs Word.

Before You Start

  • The tool accepts .docx files only. For an older .doc file, open it in Word, choose Save As and pick .docx.
  • Use common fonts such as Arial or Times New Roman for the most reliable look.
  • Remove any password from the document first.
  • Read the document once more. Fix spelling and spacing in Word, because you cannot edit the text after conversion.

Step 1: Convert the File

  1. Open Word to PDF.
  2. Click “Choose DOCX File” and select your document. Its name appears under the button.
  3. Click “Convert to PDF”.
  4. When the message “Your PDF has been created successfully” appears, the PDF downloads with the same name as your Word file.

A Simple Example

Imagine you have a two-page CV in Word with a heading, a bullet list of skills and a short table of work experience. You open the tool, choose the .docx file, press Convert, and a PDF with the same file name downloads. You open it, check that the list and the table look right, and then upload it to the job portal.

Step 2: Check the Formatting

PaperDesk reads the text, headings, lists, tables and images of the document and places them on A4 pages. Simple documents such as letters, CVs and reports convert best. Look for these problems:

  • Text boxes, headers, footers and columns may look different from Word.
  • A page break can fall in the middle of a line.
  • Special fonts may be replaced.

Important: The Text Becomes an Image

The pages are created as images inside the PDF, so the text cannot be selected, copied or searched. For a CV or assignment that only needs to be read or printed, this is usually fine. Keep your Word file for any later edits.

Tips for a Cleaner Result

  • Keep the layout simple: headings, paragraphs, lists and plain tables work best.
  • Avoid text boxes and complicated columns when you can.
  • Use A4 page size in Word, because the PDF pages are A4.
  • Do not use tiny font sizes. Small text can look soft after conversion.
  • Save a copy of the Word file before you convert, so you can change it and convert again.

Check the PDF Before You Send

  • Scroll through every page and confirm nothing is cut off.
  • Zoom in on small text, tables and images.
  • Check that bullet points and numbers are correct.
  • Compare the file size with the limit of the form or email.

If the PDF Is Too Large

Use Compress PDF. Our guide on meeting an upload size limit explains the levels. If the file is for email, read how to send a large PDF by email. To join the PDF with other documents, such as certificates, use Merge PDF.

Common Problems

  • “Something went wrong”. The file may be damaged, password-protected or not a real .docx. Save a fresh copy in Word and try again.
  • Layout looks different. Simplify the document, remove text boxes, and convert again.
  • The file is not accepted. Make sure it ends with .docx, not .doc.
  • The PDF is large. Documents with many images create larger PDFs. Compress the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert an old .doc file?

Not directly. Open it in Word, choose Save As, pick .docx, and then convert that file.

Why can't I select the text in the PDF?

The pages are created as images, so the text cannot be selected, copied or searched. Keep your Word file for edits.

Will my layout stay the same?

Simple documents convert best. Text boxes, headers, footers and columns may look different, so check the PDF before you send it.

Is my document uploaded to a server?

No. PaperDesk works inside your browser. See our Privacy Policy.
