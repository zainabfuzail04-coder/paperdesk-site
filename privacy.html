Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PaperDesk handles information when you use our website and browser-based PDF tools.

1. About PaperDesk

PaperDesk provides online PDF and document tools designed to help users perform common file-related tasks directly through a web browser. The website is available at paperdesktool.com.

2. File Processing

PaperDesk's browser-based tools are designed to process supported files locally in your web browser.

Files that you select for supported tools are not intentionally uploaded to a PaperDesk server by the tool's browser-based processing.

You are responsible for keeping backups of important files before using any online tool.

3. Website Hosting

PaperDesk is hosted using Vercel, and the website's source code is stored on GitHub. Like other websites, the hosting infrastructure may process technical information associated with requests to the website, such as IP address, browser information, device information, and request data.

Such information may be used by the hosting infrastructure for security, reliability, and operational purposes.

4. Third-Party Services and Libraries

Some PaperDesk tools may use third-party JavaScript libraries or resources delivered through content delivery networks (CDNs).

When your browser loads these resources, the relevant third-party service may receive standard technical information associated with the request.

5. Cookies and Google Analytics

PaperDesk may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality and analytics.

PaperDesk uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website, such as website traffic, page views, and general interaction with the site.

Google Analytics may collect information such as browser and device information, approximate location, session information, and website interactions. Google Analytics may also use first-party cookies or similar identifiers to distinguish users and sessions.

PaperDesk does not intentionally send personally identifiable information to Google Analytics.

You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting data about your visits by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

For more information about how Google handles data collected through websites using Google services, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

PaperDesk may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising partners. These advertisements help cover the cost of running the website and keep the tools free to use.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to PaperDesk or to other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to PaperDesk and/or other sites on the Internet.

Advertising cookies and similar technologies may collect information such as your IP address, browser and device information, and the pages you view. PaperDesk does not have access to the cookies placed by third-party advertisers, and their use is governed by their own privacy policies.

You can choose how ads are shown to you:

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that some parts of a website may work differently if cookies are blocked.

For more information, please review Google's Advertising Policy.

7. Personal Information

PaperDesk does not currently require users to create an account in order to use the available browser-based tools.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message. We use it only to reply to you. Please do not send private or confidential documents by email.

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into any form or service unless it is specifically required and appropriate.

8. Children's Privacy

PaperDesk is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed toward collecting personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the website.

9. Security

We aim to provide a reliable and secure website. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

PaperDesk may update this Privacy Policy when the website, tools, or services change.

Any updated version will be published on this page.
