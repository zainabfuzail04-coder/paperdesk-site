How to convert Word to PDF

  1. Choose your .docx file.
  2. Preview the content to confirm it looks right.
  3. Press "Convert to PDF" to download the result.

Complex layouts, images, or unusual formatting from the original Word file may render slightly differently than in Microsoft Word, since this uses a lightweight browser-based renderer rather than Word's own engine.
