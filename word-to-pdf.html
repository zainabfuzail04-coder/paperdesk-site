Word to PDF Converter Online

PaperDesk Word to PDF Converter turns a Word DOCX file into a PDF you can share, print or upload. You do not need Microsoft Word, any software or an account. Choose your document, click convert and your PDF downloads in a few seconds.

How to Convert Word to PDF

  1. Click “Choose DOCX File” and select your Word document.
  2. Check that the file name appears under the button.
  3. Click “Convert to PDF” and wait a few seconds.
  4. Your browser will download a PDF with the same name as your Word file.

When Is Word to PDF Useful?

  • Job applications: send your CV or cover letter as a PDF so it looks the same on every device.
  • Students: submit assignments and reports in a format that teachers can open easily.
  • Offices: share letters, proposals and notices without letting others edit them by mistake.
  • Printing: a PDF is easier to print than a Word file, because it does not depend on the fonts installed on another computer.

What to Expect from the Result

PaperDesk reads the text, headings, lists, tables and images of your document and places them on A4 pages. It is best suited for simple to medium documents such as letters, CVs, notes and reports.

Please note: the pages are created as images inside the PDF, so the text in the new PDF cannot be selected or searched. Complex Word features such as text boxes, headers and footers, columns and special fonts may not look exactly like they do in Word, and a page break can fall in the middle of a line. Always look through the PDF before sending it to someone important.

Your Files Stay on Your Device

PaperDesk does not upload your Word document to a remote server. Your own browser does the conversion, which is helpful when your document contains personal or work information. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

Tips for Better Results

  • Only .docx files are supported. If you have an older .doc file, open it in Word and choose Save As, then pick .docx.
  • Use common fonts such as Arial or Times New Roman for the most reliable look.
  • Keep images in a normal size. Very large images make the conversion slower.
  • If the PDF is too big to email, use Compress PDF.
  • Want to combine several converted files? Use Merge PDF.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PaperDesk Word to PDF free?

Yes. You can convert Word files to PDF without paying and without creating an account.

Can I convert a .doc file?

No. This tool works with .docx files only. Open your older .doc file in Word and save it as .docx first.

Will the PDF look exactly like my Word document?

Not always. Simple documents convert well, but complex layouts may look different. Check the PDF after converting.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. The conversion happens inside your browser, so your document stays on your device.

Why do I see “Something went wrong”?

The file may be damaged, password-protected or not a real .docx file. Try opening it in Word first, save a fresh copy, and convert that copy.
