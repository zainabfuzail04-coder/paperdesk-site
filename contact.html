How to Get a Faster Answer

If you are reporting a problem with a tool, please include the following in your message. It helps us find and fix the issue quickly.

  • The name of the tool you were using, for example Merge PDF or Compress PDF
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead
  • Your browser and device, for example Chrome on Windows or Safari on iPhone
  • Please do not send private or confidential documents by email
