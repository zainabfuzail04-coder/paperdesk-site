Split PDF Online

PaperDesk Split PDF lets you extract selected pages or page ranges from a PDF and create a new PDF document online. The tool works directly in your browser for simple PDF page extraction.

Extract Pages from a PDF

Select a PDF file and enter the page numbers or ranges you want to extract. For example, you can enter 1, 3, 5-7 to extract specific pages from your PDF.

How to Split a PDF

  1. Click the “Choose PDF File” button.
  2. Select the PDF you want to split.
  3. Enter the page numbers or page ranges.
  4. Click “Split PDF”.
  5. Download your new PDF file.

Extract Specific PDF Pages

PaperDesk allows you to extract individual pages or a range of pages from a PDF. You can select pages such as 1, 3, 5-7 and create a new PDF containing only those pages.
