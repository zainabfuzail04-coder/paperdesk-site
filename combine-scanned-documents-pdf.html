How to Combine Scanned Documents into One PDF

By Zainab Fuzail · Updated November 2026

Scans often arrive as separate files: one page from a scanner app, a photo of a certificate, a PDF of an ID. Many offices want everything in one PDF. This guide shows how to combine scans and photos into a single file in your browser.

When Do You Need This?

  • Job applications: CV, certificates and ID copy in one file. See our guide on preparing a job application PDF.
  • Admission and scholarship forms: result cards, fee receipts and photos in a single upload.
  • Office claims: several bills or receipts joined into one document.
  • Personal records: keep related papers together, such as all pages of one agreement.

Before You Start

  • Gather all your files in one folder on your computer or phone.
  • Rename them with numbers (01, 02, 03) in the order you want.
  • Check that each scan is upright and readable. Rescan blurry pages now, not later.
  • If a document is a Word file, convert it first with Word to PDF.

Step 1: Turn Photos Into PDFs

If some of your scans are pictures, convert them first with Image to PDF. It accepts JPG and PNG only, so convert HEIC or WEBP photos to JPG first. Each image goes on its own A4 page, centered and scaled to fit. In my test, a PNG screenshot of 201.5 KB became a PDF of 198 KB.

Step 2: Merge Everything

  1. Open Merge PDF.
  2. Click “Choose PDF Files” and add your PDFs. You can add more files later.
  3. Check the order in the list. Files are joined from top to bottom.
  4. Click “Merge PDFs”. A file named paperdesk-merged.pdf downloads.

When I joined a 6,789.4 KB PDF with a 5.4 KB PDF, the merged file showed as 6.6 MB in Chrome’s download list, which matches the two sizes added together (about 6,795 KB).

A Simple Example

Imagine you need to submit a scholarship file made of a scanned result card (PDF), a photo of your ID (JPG) and a fee receipt (PNG). You convert the JPG and the PNG with Image to PDF, name the three PDFs 01, 02 and 03, and add them to Merge PDF in that order. You download one file, check every page, and upload it.

Fixing the Order

There is no drag and drop. To move a file, remove it with the ✕ button and add it again, and it goes to the end. Numbering your file names (01, 02, 03) saves time. A simple trick is to add the files one by one in the exact order you need.

Tips for Better Scans

  • Scan in good light and keep the page flat, so edges are straight.
  • Use a scanner app if you have one. It usually gives cleaner pages than a plain photo.
  • Do not scan at the highest quality unless needed. Very large scans make a very large PDF.
  • Keep the same page orientation where possible, so the final file looks tidy.
  • Check that signatures and stamps are clear before you merge.

If the Result Is Too Large

Scans add up quickly. Use Compress PDF on the merged file. Our guide on meeting an upload size limit explains the levels. If the file is for email, read how to send a large PDF by email. You can also keep only the pages that are required with Split PDF.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Merging before converting photos. Convert pictures to PDF first, then merge.
  • Ignoring the order. Check the list before you click Merge.
  • Sending HEIC photos. The image tool accepts JPG and PNG only.
  • Compressing too early. Merge first, then compress the final file once.
  • Deleting the originals. Keep them until your upload is accepted.

Check Before You Send

  • Scroll through every page and confirm the order.
  • Check that each page is upright and readable.
  • Keep the original files until your upload is accepted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I mix photos and PDFs in one file?

Yes. Convert the photos to PDF with Image to PDF first, then join everything with Merge PDF.

Will the pages change when I merge?

No. Merge PDF copies the pages as they are, so the merged size is roughly the sum of the files.

Are my scans uploaded anywhere?

No. PaperDesk works inside your browser. See our Privacy Policy.

What if a scan is password-protected?

Password-protected PDFs cannot be merged. Remove the password first, then try again.
