How to Prepare Documents for an Online Job Application as PDF

By Zainab Fuzail · Updated November 2026

Job portals often ask for a single PDF: your CV, a cover letter and sometimes certificates. A messy file can cost you attention before anyone reads it. This guide shows how to prepare clean PDFs, join them in the right order and keep the size within the portal’s limit.

Which Documents Do You Usually Need?

  • CV or resume: one or two pages with your experience, education and skills.
  • Cover letter: a short letter for this specific job.
  • Certificates and degrees: copies of your qualifications, if the portal asks for them.
  • ID copy: only when the employer requires it. Never send it if nobody asked.
  • Experience letters: proof of earlier jobs, when requested.

Step 1: Read the Requirements

  • Check whether the form wants one file or separate files.
  • Note the size limit next to the upload button.
  • Check the accepted format. Most portals accept PDF.
  • Check whether the employer asks for a particular file name or order.

Step 2: Make Your CV a PDF

If your CV is a Word file, the best way is usually “Save as PDF” in Word itself, because the text stays selectable. Some employers use systems that read the text of a CV, and a PDF made of page images cannot be read that way. PaperDesk Word to PDF creates pages as images, so use it only when Word is not available, and read our guide on converting Word to PDF without surprises.

Step 3: Turn Certificates Into PDFs

Photograph or scan each certificate flat, in good light. Then use Image to PDF. It accepts JPG and PNG, and each picture goes on its own A4 page. Our guide on combining scanned documents shows the steps. On a phone, read how to turn photos into one PDF for tips on clear photos.

Step 4: Join Everything in the Right Order

  1. Open Merge PDF.
  2. Add the cover letter first, then the CV, then the certificates.
  3. Check the order in the list. Files are joined from top to bottom.
  4. Click “Merge PDFs” and download paperdesk-merged.pdf.

Rename the file clearly, for example “Ayesha-Khan-CV.pdf”, instead of leaving the default name. If you work only on a phone, our guide on merging PDF files on Android and iPhone shows the steps.

A Simple Example

Imagine you are applying for an office job. You save your CV as a PDF from Word, write a one-page cover letter, and photograph your degree and two certificates. You convert the photos with Image to PDF, add the cover letter, CV and certificates to Merge PDF in that order, and download one file. You check every page, rename it with your name, and upload it.

Step 5: Keep the Size Within the Limit

Scans make a PDF large quickly. If the file is above the limit, use Compress PDF. In my test, a practice PDF of 6,789.4 KB became 541 KB with the Recommended level. Read our guide on meeting an upload size limit before you start. If only some pages are needed, keep just those with Split PDF, and see our guide on extracting a single page. If you plan to email the application, read how to send a large PDF by email.

Tips for a Professional File

  • Use a clear file name with your name, for example “Ayesha-Khan-Application.pdf”.
  • Keep the CV short and put the most important points on the first page.
  • Make sure all scans are upright, readable and not cut off.
  • Send only the documents the employer asked for.
  • Open the final file on your phone and on a computer to be sure it looks right on both.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Wrong order. Check the list in Merge PDF before you download.
  • Blurry certificates. Retake photos in good light instead of sending unreadable pages.
  • Compressing too hard. Dates, names and stamps must stay readable.
  • Sending private documents nobody asked for. Share only what is required.
  • Uploading at the last minute. Portals can be slow near the deadline. Prepare the file a day early.
  • Deleting the originals. Keep them until the application is submitted.

Final Checklist

  • Scroll through every page and check the order.
  • Zoom in on dates, names and stamps.
  • Remove pages that are not needed.
  • Keep the original files until the application is submitted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I send one PDF or separate files?

Follow the form. If it accepts only one file, merge everything. If it has separate upload boxes, send each document on its own.

Which should I convert first, the CV or the certificates?

The order of conversion does not matter. What matters is the order in the final merged file: cover letter, CV, then certificates.

Why does Word to PDF not keep my text selectable?

PaperDesk creates the pages as images. If you need selectable text, use “Save as PDF” inside Word.

Are my documents uploaded to a server?

No. PaperDesk works inside your browser. See our Privacy Policy.
