About PaperDesk

PaperDesk is a simple collection of browser-based PDF and document tools designed to make everyday file tasks easier.

What Is PaperDesk?

PaperDesk provides easy-to-use tools for working with PDF files and documents directly from your web browser. You do not need to install any software or create an account. Open a tool, choose your file, and download the result.

Our goal is to keep these tools simple, accessible, and convenient for everyday users such as students, job seekers, and office workers.

Why PaperDesk Was Built

As a student, I often needed to merge notes, send only a few pages of a document, reduce the size of a file for an upload, or turn photos into a PDF. Again and again I ran into the same problems: heavy software, websites with confusing steps, and tools that asked for an account or uploaded my files before I could use them.

PaperDesk was created to solve these everyday problems. The aim is to give people, especially students and anyone doing quick document work, a clean and straightforward place to finish these tasks in a few clicks.

About the Creator

PaperDesk is created and maintained by Zainab Fuzail, a student of Artificial Intelligence based in Lahore, Pakistan. I built the tools to run inside the browser so that people can keep their files on their own device, and I keep improving the website based on the problems real users face.

If you have a question, a problem with a tool, or an idea for a new one, I would be glad to hear from you on the Contact page.

Our Tools

PaperDesk currently offers six tools:

  • Merge PDF joins several PDF files into one document.
  • Split PDF extracts selected pages or page ranges into a new PDF.
  • Compress PDF reduces the size of a PDF for easier sharing.
  • Word to PDF converts DOCX files into PDF documents.
  • Image to PDF turns JPG and PNG images into a PDF.
  • PDF to Image saves PDF pages as PNG images.

Browser-Based Processing

PaperDesk tools process your files directly in your browser. Your documents are not uploaded to a remote server to be converted. This keeps things convenient, and it is helpful when your files contain personal or work information.

Being Honest About Limits

We want you to know what to expect from each tool. Compress PDF and Word to PDF turn pages into images, so the text in the new PDF cannot be selected or searched, and complex Word layouts may look a little different. Each tool page explains this, and we always suggest keeping a copy of your original file.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide useful, accessible, and easy-to-use document tools for people who need quick PDF solutions, and to be clear and honest about how those tools work.
