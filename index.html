Free Online PDF Tools for Everyday Work

PDF files are used everywhere: for assignments, job applications, invoices, forms and official documents. Many everyday tasks, such as joining two files or making a file smaller, should not need expensive software. PaperDesk gives you six simple tools in one place, and each one runs in your web browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

How PaperDesk Works

  1. Open the tool you need from the list above.
  2. Choose your file from your computer or phone.
  3. Press the button and wait a few seconds.
  4. Download your new file.

What Each Tool Does

  • Merge PDF joins several PDF files into one document, in the order you see in the list.
  • Split PDF extracts the pages you choose, such as 1, 3 or 5-7, into a new PDF.
  • Compress PDF makes a PDF smaller so it is easier to email or upload. It works especially well for scans and photo-heavy files.
  • Word to PDF turns a .docx file into a PDF you can share or print.
  • Image to PDF places JPG and PNG pictures on separate pages of one PDF.
  • PDF to Image saves each page of a PDF as a PNG picture.

Who Uses PaperDesk?

  • Students who need to join notes, send only some pages, or turn photos of assignments into a PDF.
  • Job seekers who need a CV and certificates in one clean PDF.
  • Office workers who share reports, invoices and letters every day.
  • Anyone filling an online form that accepts only PDF files or has a small file size limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are PaperDesk tools free?

Yes. You can use all the tools without paying and without creating an account.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your files are processed inside your browser, so your documents stay on your device.

Do I need to install any software?

No. PaperDesk works in a modern web browser on a computer, tablet or phone.

Will the result always look perfect?

Merge, Split and Image to PDF keep your content as it is. Compress PDF and Word to PDF turn pages into images, so text in the new file cannot be selected, and complex Word layouts may look a little different. Each tool page explains this clearly, and we suggest keeping your original file.

How can I contact PaperDesk?

Visit our Contact page to send us a question or suggestion.

Your Files, Your Privacy.

PaperDesk is designed with privacy in mind. Where browser-based processing is used, your files can be processed directly on your device instead of being uploaded to a server. Read our Privacy Policy to learn more.
