Your files are processed locally on your device using JavaScript. They are never uploaded anywhere, which also means they never touch our servers.

How to merge PDF files online

  1. Click the box above and choose the PDF files you want to combine.
  2. Check the order in the list — the files will be joined top to bottom.
  3. Press "Merge PDFs" and your combined file will download automatically.

This tool works fully offline once the page has loaded, since the merging happens on your own device rather than a remote server. That makes it fast and keeps private documents private.
