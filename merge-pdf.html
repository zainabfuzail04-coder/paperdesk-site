Merge PDF Files Online for Free

PaperDesk Merge PDF lets you join several PDF documents into a single file without installing any software. Pick your files, check the order, and download one combined PDF. Everything happens inside your browser, so the tool works on a laptop, a tablet or a phone with an up-to-date browser.

How to Merge PDF Files

  1. Click the “Choose PDF Files” button and select your PDFs. You can pick several files at once or add more later.
  2. Look at the list that appears. Files are merged from top to bottom, exactly as shown.
  3. If you added a wrong file, press the ✕ button next to it to remove it.
  4. Click “Merge PDFs” and wait a few seconds while the pages are combined.
  5. Your browser will download a file named paperdesk-merged.pdf.

When Is Merging PDFs Useful?

Combining PDFs saves time whenever a document is split across several files. Some common examples:

  • Students: join assignment pages, scanned notes and a cover page into one submission file.
  • Job seekers: put your CV, cover letter and certificates into a single application PDF.
  • Offices and small businesses: merge invoices, receipts and reports before sending them to a client or accountant.
  • Everyday paperwork: bundle ID copies, forms and supporting documents for an online application that accepts only one upload.

Your Files Stay on Your Device

PaperDesk does not upload your PDFs to a remote server for merging. The pages are read and combined by your own browser, and the final file is created on your device. This is helpful when your documents contain personal or work information. You can read more in our Privacy Policy.

Tips for Better Results

  • Rename your files with numbers (01, 02, 03) so they are easy to recognise in the list.
  • Password-protected PDFs cannot be merged. Remove the password first, then try again.
  • Very large files may take longer, because your device does the work. Close other heavy tabs if the page feels slow.
  • If the merged file is too big to email, use our Compress PDF tool afterwards.
  • Need only some pages? Use Split PDF first, then merge the parts you want.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PaperDesk Merge PDF free?

Yes. You can merge PDF files without paying and without creating an account.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

No. The merging happens inside your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Can I change the order of the files?

Files are merged in the order shown in the list. To place a file last, remove it with the ✕ button and add it again.

Is there a limit on the number of files?

There is no fixed limit in the tool. The practical limit depends on your device memory and the size of your PDFs.

Why do I see an error when merging?

The file may be damaged, password-protected or not a real PDF. Try opening it in a PDF viewer first, or remove the protection and try again.
