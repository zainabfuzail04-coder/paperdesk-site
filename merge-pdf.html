PaperDesk Merge PDF lets you combine multiple PDF files into one document online. Select two or more PDF files and merge them into a single PDF directly in your browser.

Combine PDF Files into One

Use this PDF merger to combine multiple documents into one organized PDF file. Your selected PDF files are processed directly in your browser without uploading them to a remote server.

How to Merge PDF Files

  1. Click the “Choose PDF Files” button.
  2. Select the PDF files you want to combine.
  3. Review the selected files and their order.
  4. Click “Merge PDFs”.
  5. Download your merged PDF file.
